A FEW THINGS
BEFORE YOU GO.

How does an invite link work?+

Create an event, then share its URL with your friends. Try the demo now to create a link with a name, date, and location. The full app will add saved events and RSVP tracking.

Will my friends need an account?+

The planned RSVP flow supports Google or email sign-in. Account authentication is still to come in this homepage demo.

Is there really a free plan?+

The Hangout is the proposed free tier. Three optional paid tiers are shown above with draft pricing and features. Subscriptions and payments are not enabled yet.

What kind of events can I make?+

Anything worth getting together for. Birthday drinks, dinner with friends, weekend walks, club meetups, or a spontaneous coffee. You bring the idea.
